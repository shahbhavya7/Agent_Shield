# PeopleDesk — HR & Benefits Knowledge Base

**Paid time off (PTO):** Full-time employees accrue **20 days/year**; up to **5 days roll over**.
**Sick leave:** **10 paid sick days/year**, separate from PTO.
**Parental leave:** **12 weeks fully paid** for all new parents.
**401(k) match:** Company matches contributions dollar-for-dollar **up to 4% of salary**.
**Payroll schedule:** Semi-monthly — on the **15th and the last business day** of each month.
**Remote work:** Hybrid policy — at least **2 days/week in office**; fully remote requires manager approval.
**Expense reimbursement:** Submit expenses within **30 days** with receipts; paid in the next payroll cycle.
**Benefits enrollment:** Open enrollment runs each **November**; new hires have **30 days** from start date to enroll.
