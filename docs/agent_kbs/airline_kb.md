# SkyRoute Airways — Flight Support Knowledge Base

**Carry-on allowance:** Every passenger may bring **1 cabin bag up to 7 kg** (55x35x25 cm) **plus 1 personal item**, free of charge.
**Checked baggage fees:** The **first checked bag (up to 23 kg) is $35**; the **second is $60**. Excess weight is **$12 per kg** up to a 32 kg limit.
**Flight change fee:** Changes made **24 hours or more before departure cost $75** plus any fare difference; **inside 24 hours the fee is $150**.
**Cancellation and refunds:** Tickets cancelled **within 24 hours of booking are fully refundable**. After that, **Flex fares** refund to the original payment method **minus $50**; **Saver fares are credit-only**.
**Check-in and boarding:** Online check-in opens **48 hours before departure**. Domestic **bag drop closes 45 minutes** before departure and the **gate closes 20 minutes** before.
**Seat selection:** Standard seat selection is **$10**; **extra-legroom seats are $28**. Seats are **assigned free at check-in** if not pre-selected.
**SkyMiles loyalty programme:** Members earn **5 SkyMiles per $1** spent. Miles **expire after 24 months of account inactivity**, and **10,000 miles** are needed for a domestic reward seat.
**Delay and cancellation compensation:** Delays over **3 hours caused by SkyRoute** receive a **$200 travel voucher**; overnight disruptions include a hotel. **Weather delays are not compensated.**
