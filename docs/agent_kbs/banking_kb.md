# NorthBank — Retail Banking Knowledge Base

**Transfer limits:** Domestic **wire transfers up to $25,000/day**; **ACH transfers up to $10,000/day**.
**Overdraft fee:** **$35 per item**, maximum **3 fees per day**.
**Card replacement:** A replacement debit card arrives in **5–7 business days**; expedited shipping is **2 days for $25**.
**Fraud reporting:** Report suspected fraud **24/7 at 1-800-NORTHBK**; disputed charges are provisionally credited within **10 business days**.
**Loan rates:** Personal loans start at **9.99% APR**; auto loans start at **6.49% APR** for qualified applicants.
**Mobile deposit:** Mobile check deposits are limited to **$10,000/day**; funds are available in **1–2 business days**.
**Statements:** Statements close on the **last day of each month** and are available online for **7 years**.
**Support hours:** Phone support **7am–9pm daily**; branches open **9am–5pm Mon–Sat**.
