# ShopEasy — Customer Support Knowledge Base

**Returns:** Items may be returned within **30 days** of delivery for a full refund.
**Refund processing:** Refunds are processed within **5–7 business days** after we receive the item.
**Shipping:** Shipping is **free on orders over $50**; below $50 a **$5 flat fee** applies.
**Warranty:** All products carry a **1-year manufacturer warranty** covering defects.
**Support hours:** Customer support is available **9am–5pm, Monday–Friday**.
**Order status:** Order status can be looked up with an order number; most orders **ship in 1–2 days**.
**Cancellations:** Orders can be cancelled free of charge **within 1 hour** of being placed.
**Payment methods:** We accept **major credit cards, PayPal, and Apple Pay**.
