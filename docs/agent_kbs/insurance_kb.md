# SafeGuard — Auto & Home Insurance Claims Knowledge Base

**Claim filing window:** Claims must be filed within **30 days** of the incident; photos and a police report (if applicable) speed processing.
**Deductible:** Standard **collision deductible $500**; **comprehensive $250**.
**Claim processing time:** Approved claims are processed and paid within **10–15 business days** of documentation.
**Roadside assistance:** Available **24/7 at 1-800-SAFEGRD**; covers **towing up to 50 miles**.
**Glass repair:** Windshield **chip repair is covered with no deductible**; full replacement is subject to your deductible.
**Rental car coverage:** Reimburses up to **$30/day** for a maximum of **30 days** while your car is repaired.
**Premium grace period:** There is a **10-day grace period** for premium payments before coverage lapses.
**Total loss valuation:** Total-loss vehicles are settled at **actual cash value (ACV)** based on comparable market listings.
