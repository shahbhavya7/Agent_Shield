# ConnectWave — Mobile Customer Support Knowledge Base

**Plan pricing:** **Essential $25/month for 10 GB**, **Plus $40/month for 50 GB**, **Unlimited $60/month**.
**Data overage:** Going over your allowance costs **$10 per additional GB**. Speeds are reduced to **512 kbps after 3 extra GB**.
**International roaming:** **$12 per day** in **90 countries**, capped at **$120 per billing cycle**. Must be **enabled before you travel**.
**Billing and late payment:** Bills are issued on the **1st** and due on the **15th**. Payments more than **10 days late incur a $15 fee**; service is **suspended after 30 days**.
**SIM replacement and eSIM:** A replacement physical SIM is **$10** and arrives in **3–5 business days**. An **eSIM is free** and activates within **15 minutes**.
**Contract and cancellation:** Contracts run **12 months**. Cancelling early costs **$20 per remaining month**, and **30 days notice** is required.
**Network coverage:** **5G covers 82%** of the country and **4G covers 97%**. Reported outages are resolved within **4 hours** on average.
**Device upgrades:** You can upgrade **after 9 months** of on-time payments. Trade-in credit is applied to your **next 12 bills**.
